# Code of Conduct

Nysera is a small, friendly open-source project. We want everyone who
takes part — in issues, pull requests and discussions — to feel welcome.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent and ask before judging.
- Respect different opinions, skill levels and backgrounds.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory jokes or language
- Posting someone's private information without permission
- Spam, scams or repeated off-topic promotion

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, or block
accounts that break these rules. To report a problem privately, use
GitHub's **Report content** option or contact the maintainers through the
repository's Security tab.

Thanks for helping keep Nysera a good place to build together.
